## Overview

I served as a Data Entry Intern at Mars Computer from **November 2022 to April 2023**. During this period, I was responsible for various administrative and data-management tasks that ensured smooth daily operations.

## Key Responsibilities

- **Input total income and sold items from customer receipts into Excel**, maintaining accurate daily sales records.
- **Save and archive documents related to the entered data** for easy future access and auditing purposes.
- **Create reports or summaries** from the entered data to facilitate further business analysis and decision-making.
- **Upload product content** for sale on the marketplace, ensuring product listings were up to date and accurate.

## Outcome

Through this internship, I developed a strong attention to detail, proficiency in data management tools like Excel, and a deeper understanding of digital marketplace administration.
