## Overview

I serve as a **Master Trainer** for the **AI Ready ASEAN** initiative by the **ASEAN Foundation** (September 2025 – September 2026). My primary focus is to advance digital literacy across the region by educating participants on core concepts and the ethical use of Artificial Intelligence.

## Key Responsibilities & Achievements

- **Prepared structured learning materials** and conducted technical documentation to support training delivery.
- **Delivered training sessions** on the Fundamentals of Generative AI, AI Ethics & Safety, and AI applications for digital literacy.
- **Taught participants** how to apply AI responsibly, specifically addressing challenges such as misinformation and digital safety.

## Outcome

This ongoing initiative significantly contributes to building a resilient, AI-literate community across ASEAN, equipping individuals with the essential knowledge and ethical framework necessary in today's digital landscape.
