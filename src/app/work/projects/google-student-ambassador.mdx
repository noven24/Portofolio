## Overview

I served as a **Google Student Ambassador** at **Universitas Muhammadiyah Purwokerto** (September 2025 – December 2025). During this period, I acted as the main point of contact between students, faculty, and Google teams, presenting Google technologies at various campus events.

## Key Achievements & Responsibilities

- **Outstanding Recognition**: Selected as a "Rising Star" (Top 200 out of 800 nationwide candidates) and successfully graduated in the Top 100 for actively driving tech initiatives and student engagement on campus.
- **Event Organization**: Independently organized and facilitated 2 offline tech training events, successfully engaging 20 participants to foster hands-on digital skills and Google tech adoption.
- **Tech Liaison**: Served as the main point of contact between students, faculty, and Google teams, effectively introducing and presenting Google technologies at various university events.

## Outcome

Through this role, I strengthened my leadership and public speaking skills while driving meaningful digital transformation and tech fluency among my university peers.
