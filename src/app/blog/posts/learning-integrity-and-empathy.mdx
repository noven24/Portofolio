As an Informatics Engineering student, my daily life is usually filled with lines of code, algorithm design, and complex problem-solving behind the glow of a monitor. Recently, however, I decided to step out of that comfort zone and engage directly with the community.

I had the opportunity to volunteer as an Usher at the Anti-Corruption Film Festival held in Purbalingga. This festival is not just an ordinary cinema appreciation event; it is a movement aimed at raising critical public awareness about the issues of corruption that surround us.

On paper, the duties of an Usher might sound simple. My work centered around welcoming and assisting attendees, directing them to appropriate seating, and providing accurate information regarding film screening schedules and interactive discussion sessions.

However, the reality on the ground provided lessons that went far deeper than merely pointing out empty chairs.

Being on the front lines of this event pushed me to train my empathy and proactive communication skills. Interacting with hundreds of visitors from diverse backgrounds—ranging from students and artists to the general public—taught me the art of "understanding the user" firsthand. In the world of technology and UI/UX that I study, we are constantly reminded to empathize with the user. This festival was a tangible manifestation of that empathy.

Through this role, I learned how crucial active public participation is in the fight against corruption. Transparency and integrity cannot stand by relying solely on regulations or sophisticated systems; they require humans who care and are willing to engage in dialogue. Connecting with individuals who share the same vision and passion at this event truly ignited my optimism.

Ultimately, this experience in Purbalingga serves as a powerful reminder for my future career journey. The technological innovations I build—whether they are web systems or Artificial Intelligence (AI)—must be grounded in high integrity. Great technology isn't just computationally smart; it is technology that has a "soul" and a real purpose to bring transparency and a positive impact to society at large.
